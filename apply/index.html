Describe a configuration. Compose its conformant classification statement.

This instrument does not score, certify, rank, or judge any vendor. It classifies the configuration you describe — one vehicle or fleet, one export path, one site, one jurisdiction, at one point in time — by the rule set in §4 of the standard: a configuration holds the highest level whose boundary condition, and all lower boundary conditions, it satisfies.

A statement that omits its configuration description, its site/jurisdiction, or its date is not conformant (§6). A statement applied to a brand or product in general — rather than a described configuration — is not conformant.
