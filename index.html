READING THE SCALE
No scores. States.
The six dimensions carry no weights and produce no index. They are examination headings — a configuration holds the highest O-level whose conditions it fully satisfies.
Canonical Definition · Outrush Readiness Standard v1.0
Outrush Readiness is the actual-readiness layer for reverse energy flow from electric vehicles — the measured distance between declared capability and a flow that is permitted, supported, safe, directed, operated, and economically governed.
V2X tells you what is possible. Outrush Readiness tells you what is ready.
This standard does not compete with V2G, V2H, V2X, ISO 15118, or OCPP. Those define directions, interfaces, and protocols. This standard sits above them and classifies the one thing they do not: the distance between a capability printed on a specification sheet and an energy flow that actually runs — lawfully, safely, and under settled economics — at a specific site, today.
Scope
Specification only. The standard classifies states. It does not measure, score, certify, audit, or rank.
Separation
Classify the configuration, not the vendor. A classification describes a configuration as stated — never a company, brand, or product as such.
Precedence
OUTRUSH_READINESS_STANDARD.md governs. Where any page or summary conflicts with the standard, the standard wins.
The O-Scale · Six States of Outrush Readiness
LevelStateBoundary condition — what must be trueWhat it does not imply
O0 Declared Capability Export capability is claimed by manufacturer or specification sheet. Nothing beyond the claim is established. That any export path exists, is permitted, or has ever operated.
O1 Hardware Path A complete physical export path exists — vehicle, connector, and export-capable supply equipment installed and compatible at the site. That exporting energy at this site is lawful or authorized.
O2 Permitted Flow The authorization layer is settled — interconnection permission, utility agreement, or regulatory allowance covers this configuration at this site and jurisdiction. That the destination, protections, or metering of the flow are defined.
O3 Directed Flow The destination is explicitly defined and configured — home, building, grid, emergency load, or fleet — with the protection and metering that destination requires. That the flow is managed within battery, safety, and scheduling constraints over time.
O4 Operated Flow The flow runs under active operational management — battery-impact limits including warranty terms, scheduling, safety interlocks, and monitoring in force. That anyone is accountable for the economics, or that the flow is compensated.
O5 Governed Flow A contract or tariff defines compensation or settlement, and operational and economic risks are explicitly allocated between parties. A permanent status. Any change in equipment, permission, destination, operation, or contract re-opens the classification.
Reading of the scale O0–O1 are capability states. O2–O3 are authorization and definition states. O4–O5 are operation and governance states. The industry's marketing language lives almost entirely at O0. The industry's working reality is decided at O2 and above.
